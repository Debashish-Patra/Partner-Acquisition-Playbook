# Partner and franchise acquisition playbook

A practical playbook for recruiting partners, franchisees or agents online with SEO and paid media. It is sector-neutral, with notes for regulated financial distribution in India.

The playbook covers the funnel and its events, a readiness checklist, the SEO and paid media workstreams, a bidding ladder, lead qualification, measurement, decision rules, compliance and a 90-day plan.

## What is inside

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `playbook/partner-acquisition-playbook.md` | The full playbook, 13 sections |
| `templates/keyword-sheet.csv` | Keywords by intent tier, landing page and rank |
| `templates/funnel-definitions.csv` | Stage definitions, time limits and owners |
| `templates/creative-log.csv` | Claims, sources and approvals for every creative |
| `templates/weekly-scorecard.csv` | Weekly funnel and cost review with target floors and goals |

## Who it is for

Marketing and growth teams at companies that grow through independent partners, where a partner needs screening or documents before starting and shows their value over months.

## How to use it

1. Start with sections 2 and 3 of the playbook. Write down your funnel stages and agree the definitions between marketing and sales.
2. Work through the readiness checklist before you spend anything.
3. Run the 90-day plan in section 10, and review weekly using section 8.
4. Copy the CSV templates into your own sheet and replace the example rows.
5. Replace every illustrative number with your own baseline after the first 30 days of data.

## Status and limits

- Every number in the playbook is a formula or an illustrative assumption. There is no benchmark data in it.
- The thresholds, such as the number of qualified leads needed before changing bidding, are rules of thumb. Platform guidance changes, so check it.
- The compliance section is a list of questions for your legal team and is not legal advice. The India notes should be verified against current rules.
